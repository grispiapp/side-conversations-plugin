- Replaced the custom DOMParser/TreeWalker sanitizer with one explicit DOMPurify policy that preserves Phase 3 formatting and quote semantics while dropping active/data-bearing element families and unsafe attributes or protocols.

- Added dedicated and integration regression coverage for hostile/provider HTML, render sinks, formatting active states, controlled selection preservation, paste, history, keyboard, focus, and disabled behavior.

## Decisions Made

- Kept all three public sanitizer helper signatures and the first-blockquote/public-only chronological quote rules source-compatible.
- Used DOMPurify’s explicit `ALLOWED_TAGS`, anchor-only `ALLOWED_ATTR`, disabled data/ARIA attributes, strict URI regex, and forbidden-content list; safe targets and relationships are added only by the reviewed wrapper.
- Used Tiptap’s transaction-driven active state and history rather than retaining any browser-command fallback.
- Used a last-emitted sanitized HTML marker to distinguish controlled parent echoes from genuine external draft restore/clear.
