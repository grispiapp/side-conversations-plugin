<objective>
Second vertical slice: give every rendered row its correct "sıra kimde" badge and put the whole list into the right order. After Plan 01 the agent sees a flat, real list; after this plan each row shows Yeni yanıt / Yanıt bekleniyor / Kapalı, new-reply rows are highlighted with the purple rail and float to the top, and the list is grouped/sorted per D-08.
